Three ways to explore.

Objects. Places. Collections.
01 / Places

Step into an ancient village.

Navigate a reconstructed Iron Age settlement and explore its interactive details. Architecture becomes a way into conversations about everyday life, homes, and the communities that inhabited them.

Historical reconstruction · Interactive web experience
Explore the village ↗
02 / Objects

Look closer. Try it yourself.

Explore a 1556 German owl beaker in 3D. Remove its head, pour a drink, and zoom into the details—revealing how an object was used as well as how it looks.

Interactive artifact · Browser-based demo
03 / Collections

Open the pages behind the glass.

A library kiosk proposal for browsing digitized rare books and exploring objects in 3D. Architectural renderings and an immersive walkthrough show how the experience could sit within the exhibition.

Concept proposal · In-gallery touchscreen

About the studio

Built around objects,
spaces, and their stories.

I'm Jake Sheykhet, an artist and designer with a background in architecture. Sheykhet Studios creates interactive digital experiences that bring collections and historical environments to life.

My work also includes digital models for museum planning, visualization, and installation, including Gallery 208 at the Jewish Museum in New York.
